Troubleshooting guide

Save timed out: Check Recent Entries and the destination before repeating the same entry.

Backend unavailable: Check connection and the configured backend before trying again.

Sync pending or unverified: Use Verify destination. Source-side Saved does not prove the copy reached HR, Analysis or MyFamily.

Photo problems: Confirm JPEG within 20 MB and verify the saved entry before another upload.

PIN or session expired: Reauthorize through SarNi; do not include credentials in reports.
